Private tools · Operated by Mark Brown

Everyday account tasks, with user control.

Mark’s MCP Tools connect an AI application to email, calendars, spreadsheets and files for tasks directed by Mark.

What the tools do

  • Search and read Gmail messages and attachments, prepare replies, and send email after review and approval.
  • Read and manage Google Calendar events.
  • Read and update Google Sheets.
  • Find, read and manage permitted Google Drive files.

Access depends on the account’s authorization and the permissions configured for each tool. The suite is privately operated for Mark’s personal accounts and organisational or delegated accounts he is explicitly authorized to use.

This is an information website. It does not ask you to sign in to Google, collect Google authorization tokens, or receive your Gmail, Calendar, Sheets or Drive content.

Review and privacy

The tools run locally. Requested account data can pass to the AI application to complete a task. Selected proposed actions can also be presented through an approved remote review service. See the privacy information for the data flow and controls.

These tools are independently operated and are not a Google product. This site makes no claim of Google verification or endorsement.
