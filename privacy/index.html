Privacy information

Mark Brown operates this private suite. Access is for user-directed tasks using personal accounts or accounts he is explicitly authorized to manage.

Account data and credentials

Depending on the task and permissions, the tools access email and attachments, calendar entries, spreadsheet cells, and Drive files or metadata. Google authorizes access through OAuth. Credentials are held in local credential storage, including macOS Keychain or local caches, depending on the tool. They are not collected by this website.

How data is used and shared

Relevant requested data passes through local tools to the AI application to answer requests or prepare actions. Approved review payloads, such as message text and selected attachments, may be sent to Mark’s remote review service at browser-handoff-relay.fly.dev.

Handling by the AI provider and review service depends on their applicable settings, terms and technical controls. This page does not promise that those services retain no data or permit no human access. Users should review those controls before authorizing sensitive tasks.

Permitted use

The suite’s policy is to use Google data only for authorized, user-directed features, and to follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data must not be sold, used for advertising or credit decisions, or used to train or improve generalized AI models. Account access does not authorize unrelated uses.

Control and deletion

Users can revoke access through their Google Account’s third-party connections. Revoking access prevents further authorized access; it does not automatically delete existing copies. For help removing local credentials or task artifacts, or identifying downstream retention and deletion controls, contact markbrownorama@gmail.com.

This static site has no sign-in form, analytics script or tracking cookies. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, under its own settings and terms.
